Medicine Categories

Explore medicines by type for easy searching

Tablets

Tablets are the most commonly used form of medicine. They are easy to store, easy to carry, and provide accurate dosage. Tablets are widely used for pain relief, fever, infections, and chronic diseases.

Syrups

Syrups are liquid medicines, mostly given to children and elderly patients who have difficulty swallowing tablets. They are commonly used for cough, cold, fever, and digestive problems.

Injections

Injections provide quick relief because the medicine directly enters the bloodstream. They are mainly used in emergencies, hospitals, and for serious infections and pain management.

Capsules

Capsules are easy to swallow and dissolve quickly in the stomach. They are commonly used for antibiotics, vitamins, and supplements.

Ointments & Creams

These medicines are applied directly on the skin for infections, wounds, burns, allergies, and pain relief. They act quickly on the affected area.

Vitamins & Supplements

Vitamins and supplements help in improving immunity, energy levels, and overall health. They are often taken for nutritional support.

Why Categories Matter

Categorizing medicines helps customers find the right product quickly and easily. Instead of searching every medicine one by one, users can simply choose a category and browse the related medicines. This improves user experience and saves time.

Benefits of Category-Based Search

  • Easy navigation for customers
  • Quick access to required medicines
  • Better organization of pharmacy stock
  • Reduces chances of confusion
  • Improves overall website usability
